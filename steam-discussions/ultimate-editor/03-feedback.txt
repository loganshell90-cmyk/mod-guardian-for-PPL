TITLE: 💬 Feedback & Suggestions - like it? hate it? tell me!

[h1]Feedback & Suggestions[/h1]
I want to know what you think of Ultimate Editor — the good [b]and[/b] the bad. Honest feedback is how the mod gets better.

[h2]Quick feedback (copy & fill in)[/h2]
[code]
What I like:
What I don't like:
What I'd change:
Feature I wish it had:
Rating (1-10):
[/code]

[h2]Suggesting a new feature?[/h2]
[list]
[*]Say [b]what[/b] it should do and [b]why[/b] it would be useful.
[*]Check if someone already suggested it — if so, reply to theirs so I can see how many people want it.
[*]Drawings or screenshots of what you mean are very welcome!
[/list]

[h2]What happens to suggestions?[/h2]
I read every one. Ideas I'm adding show up in the [b]Weekly Update[/b] posts under "Coming soon." I can't add everything, but popular ideas get priority.

[i]Not liking something is totally fine to say — just explain why so I can fix it. 🙂[/i]
